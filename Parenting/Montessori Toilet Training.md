# Montessori Toilet Training
> Toddler Community Education meeting on Wednesday, October 1st from 4:00-5:00pm. The topic will be toilet learning.

## Action Items
- Pick a weekend to go diaper-less.
- Coordinate with Montessori.
- Keep the floor potty in the room that we spend the most time in.

## Notes from Information Session
- Standup diapering
	- They pull their own pants down.
		- Thumbs on waistband.
		- Small mirror at toddler height.
	- Take their own diaper to the trash.
		- "poop goes in the toilet"
	- Flush if any *product*.
	- Tell them to go get a diaper.
- Signs and indicators that they're ready to wear underpants
	- Occasional dry diaper
	- Adult readiness
- "Soon you're going to wear underpants"
	- We need to coordinate with Montessori
- Take them every 20 to 30 minutes to the bathroom (at first)
- Catch them in between activities.
- Phrase things where there's no "yes" or "no" answers.
	- "It's time to sit on the toilet"
	- "It's your turn to sit on the toilet"
	- "Come sit on the toilet"
	- "It looks like you may need to go to the bathroom"
- Build a toileting routine into daily transitions
- This is about establishing comfort, independence, confidence, and routine.
- It's a lot of hard work.
	- It's a big job for the toddler.
- Involve the child in everything in the process. Child should be able to:
	- Wash/dry their own hands
	- Get clean clothes
	- Put dirty/wet clothes into the hamper
- Training underwear.
- Wearing just underwear makes things easier for the first few days.
	- Reduces the amount of laundry.
	- Easier to take off.
- Montessori doesn't like to use the term "accident".
	- No shaming the child.
	- "I see you have wet pants. Let's go change."

## Notes from Q/A Session
- Plan to not leave the house for a few days while toilet training.
- The more you're able to be consistent with keeping the toddler out of a diaper, the faster they'll become consistent with staying dry.
- It's important to not go back and forth.
- The first few weeks and/or months can be really, really wet.
- Adult readiness is a huge part of toilet learning.
- Have a game plan. Don't look back.
- Try not to get discouraged.
- Using diapers at naptime and bedtime can result in holding.
- Community can help progress toilet learning. Never force them.
- "Pull ups are capitalism. You don't need them for the toileting process."
- There's a lot of value in practicing sitting on the toilet and doing standup diapering.
- Keep the floor potty in the room that you spend the most time in.
- Keep them in just underpants.
- It's hard to tell if quick dry pants are wet. Just stick with standard cotton under pants.
- Acknowledge that this is a big thing that's very hard.
- We're allowed to go use the toilet before leaving school.
- Montessori focuses on intrinsic validations/acknowledgements of accomplishments.
	- No reward system.
	- No cheering and clapping.
	- No bribery.
	- "If it works for you at home, do your thing."
