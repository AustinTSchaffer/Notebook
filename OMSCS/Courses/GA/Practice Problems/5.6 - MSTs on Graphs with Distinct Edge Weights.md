# 5.6 - MSTs on Graphs with Distinct Edge Weights
> Let $G=(V,E)$ be an undirected graph. Prove that if all its edge weights are distinct, then it has a unique minimum spanning tree.

See [[5.9 - Properties of Weighted Undirected Graphs]] if you want proofs. If all edges are unique, then MST algorithms do not have to break ties when selecting which edges to add to the resulting MST. Therefore, the resulting MST will always be the same, no matter what algorithm you use.
