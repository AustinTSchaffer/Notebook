# 7.6 - Unbounded Feasible LP
> Give an example of a linear program in two variables whose feasible region is infinite, but such that there is an optimum solution of bounded cost.

Objective: $\max \space 0.5x-y$
Constraints:
- $x,y \ge 0$
- $y-2x \le 5$
- $-y+x \le 10$

I found this one by messing around in Desmos.
- The gray region is the region of feasibility.
- The lines show the integer solutions to the objective function, illustrating the gradient of the objective function.
- The green lines show "profit".
- The red lines show "loss".
- The thicker the line, the higher the absolute value.
- The optimal value occurs at $(x=10,y=0)$
