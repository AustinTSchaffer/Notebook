# 7.3 - Cargo Plane (TODO)
![[Pasted image 20260317223151.png]]

The cargo plane has a capacity. Minimum of 100 tons and 60 $m^3$. There are 3 materials which can be transported:
- M1
	- 2 tones/cubic meter
	- 40 cubic meters available
	- $1,000 per cubic meter
- M2
	- 1 ton/cubic meter
	- 30 cubic meters available
	- $1,200 per cubic meter
- M3
	- 3 tones/cubic meter
	- 20 cubic meters available
	- $12,000 per cubic meter
