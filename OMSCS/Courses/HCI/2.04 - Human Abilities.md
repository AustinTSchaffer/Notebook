# Perception

- eyes (visual perception)
	- (focus) color
	- (focues / periphery) movement
	- avoid relying on colors to convey information
	- average person's visual acuity will decrease over time
- ears
	- pitch
	- loudness
	- localization
	- not directional
	- can't easily filter out auditory information
- skin
	- can feel things
	- can feel pressure, vibration, temperature
	- hard to filter out perception

- Restricted by the sensors that are available on the hardware.
- Phone can detect what kinds of notifications to supply based on context and sensors.
- Iterate on designs.

## Memory + Time
- Perception buffer (?)
- Short-term memory
	- "chunking"
	- "four to five chunks at a time"
	- Far easier to recognize something you know, than recall it independently
	- Phone numbers are easier to remember than a string of 10 undelineated numbers.
- Long-term memory
	- lightner system (flash cards)

## Learning
- procedural learning = how to do something
- declarative learning = knowledge about something
- procedural knowledge is generally what HCI is all about
