# 3.5.R1 - What do prototypes prototype?
> Houde, S., & Hill, C. (1997). *What do prototypes prototype?* In M. Helandar, T.K. Landaeur, & P. Prabhu (Eds). *Handbook of Human-Computer Interaction, 2*. (pp. 367-381). Elsevier Science.

- core means of exploring and expressing designs for interactive computer artifacts
- represent different states of an evolving design and to explore options
- this chapter proposes changes to the language used to talk about interfaces
	- What role will it have in a user's life?
	- How should it look and feel?
	- How should it be implemented?
- refers to interfaces as "interactive computer artifacts"

## Problem with Prototypes
- look and feel
- who are the users?
### What is a prototype?
- different domains, different specializations, different views of what constitutes a "prototype"
- orgs develop their own "prototyping cultures"

### Current terminology
- focus on attributes of the prototype itself (e.g. which tool was used to create it)
- "high-fidelity" vs "low-fidelity"
- no one tool supports iterative design work in all of the important areas of investigation
