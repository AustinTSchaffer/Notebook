# 1.2 - Introduction to CS6750

- Learning Goals
	- to understand the common principles in HCI
	- understand the design lifecycle
	- to understand current applications of HCI
- Learning Outcomes
	- to design effective interactions between humans and computers.
		- Don't want to ignore decades of experience when designing new interfaces.
		- Applying known principles to known problems don't guarantee a good design.
	- Effectiveness / Effective Interactions
		- Usability
		- Research
		- Change the Activity
	- Between Humans and Computers
		- "we design interactions, not interfaces"
		- "Not designing a thermostat, we're designing a way for a person to control the temperature in their home."
- Learning Strategies
	- By example
	- By doing
	- By reflection
	- "You are not your own user!"
- GT Course Learning Strategies
	- Peer Learning
	- Collaborative Learning
	- Learning by Teaching
	- Communities of Practice
	- Project-Based Learning
- Learning Assessments
	- Design effective interfaces between humans and computers.
- Course Structure
	- Start with design
	- Cover research
- 5 tips for doing well
	- Look over assignments early
	- Start assignments early
	- Participate in the class forum
	- Select an application area to explore
	- Leave behind what you know
- Course Units
	- Unit 1 - Introduction
	- Unit 2 - Principles
	- Unit 3 - Methods
	- Unit 4 - Applications
