# 1.1 - Introduction to Human-Computer Interaction

- humans
	- seeing
	- hearing
	- feeling
	- memories
	- experiences
	- skills
	- knowledge
- computer
	- everything has computers in them these days
- interaction
	- humans interact with computers
	- computers interact back by responding
	- really the human is interacting with the task at hand, and the computer is the mediator
	- the interface should be somewhat invisible
	- the user should be able to focus on the task at hand, instead of the inteface

> We might be experts at interacting with computers, but that doesn't make us experts at designing interactions between other humans and computers.

> You might exit this class less confident in your ability to design effective interfaces.
