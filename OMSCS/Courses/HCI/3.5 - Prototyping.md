## Types of Prototypes
![[Pasted image 20250521224543.png]]

### Verbal Prototypes
- just telling the user the same thing we tell our engineers
- easy to do
- hard to do effectively
- social desirability bias
	- "uh huh, yeah that sounds really well thought out"
- Does the user understand what the heck you're talking about?
- expert blindspot
- analogies are powerful here
	- "It's the Uber of accidentally burning your house down."
### Paper Prototyping
- draw up a prototype on paper
- button layouts
- iconography
- "What do you think of this design?"
- Prompts feedback
- layout information
- incorporate ideas into the drawing or a new drawing
- can do interactive sessions
	- multiple drawings for different contexts within the app
	- this is called card-based prototyping
### Wizard of Oz Prototyping
- "Pay no attention to that man behind the curtain."
- Voice commands in your app? Pretend to be the AI that powers the voice commands.
- Test out ideas for different interactions
### Wireframing
- Use some detailed tools
- Figma
- PowerPoint
- Google Drawings
- Might be old ones that nobody uses anymore
	- balsamiq
	- axure
	- Visio
	- InDesign
	- pencil project
	- framebox
### Physical Prototypes
- doesn't have to actually work
- can take an interface that does something unrelated and pretend that it's part of your new system
### Multi-Level Prototyping
- Not every component of the interface needs to be prototyped to the same fidelity
- apps have multiple functions with different interactions
- moving between screens is itself an interaction
