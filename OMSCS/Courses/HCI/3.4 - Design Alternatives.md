# 3.4 - Design Alternatives

- design alternatives
- brainstorming
- design is hard
- infinite choices
- how to generate ideas
- how to explore ideas

![[Pasted image 20250515205438.png]]

- **Main Goal**: Generate lots of ideas.
- Explore the problem **FIRST**
- Starting out individually is often the best way to start (this is unintuitive)
- Just write out ideas in a few words or less
- messy paper is good
- don't be afraid to leave and come back
- don't cross anything out (except typos)
- moving around helps (get up and walk)
- 5 tips
	- write down the core problem and keep it visible. Stay grounded in the problem
	- constrain yourself
		- have at least 1 idea in many different categories
		- rule out expensive/impossible ideas (?)
	- aim for 20 ideas
	- take a break (stop brainstorming, come back later)
	- divide and conquer
- issues with group brainstorming
	- social loafing. Tendency to work less when in a group.
	- conformity. People in groups tend to have convergent thinking
	- production blocking. some people may dominate conversation
	- performance matching. people converge in passion and performance, leading to a loss in performance over time
	- power dynamics and biases
- rules for group brainstorming
	- expressiveness
	- non-evaluation
	- quantity > quality
	- building
	- stay focused
	- no explaining ideas (say idea and move on)
	- revisit the problem often
	- encourage others
	- every individual must do these
- 5 tips (design of the activity)
	- go through every individual idea
	- find the optimal size. Social lurking occurs when someone can hide in the crowd.
	- set clear rules for communication
	- set clear expectations
	- end with ideas, not decisions

## Flesh out the Ideas
- explore ideas
- dismiss ideas
- create personas
	- create many DnD characters
	- there should be a range of diversity in these personas
	- personas should represent stakeholders
- design for a specific set of user attribute ranges
- make sure it's feasible to design for the breadth of selected ranges
